GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. XDA reports that NotRexed, a lead developer on Hookshot (the static-recompilation project that brought the Switch 2 Ocarina of Time remake to PC), has announced an unofficial GTA 6 PC project called Leonida on Discord. The pitch is that the Xbox Series CPU is x86-64 like a PC, so the game code can run natively. GPU shaders get translated to SPIR-V for Vulkan, and assets are read from a disc image you dump from your own copy. The team says it has about 75 contributors and help from Claude, and that it has already reimplemented the console's system software. That's their claim, not something XDA verified.

What it doesn't do yet is boot. Leonida ships no game files or keys, and it needs GTA 6's executable, which isn't in the roughly 209GB leaked Xbox build that Reddit users have been passing around. The leaker says the executable will come out when a countdown ends, or earlier if their cryptocurrency reaches a $15 million market cap. XDA calls that a scam, and it's a good reason to stay away from it. If you want GTA 6 on PC, the realistic wait is still for Rockstar. Rumors point to 6 to 12 months out, which would roughly match the gap GTA V had.
