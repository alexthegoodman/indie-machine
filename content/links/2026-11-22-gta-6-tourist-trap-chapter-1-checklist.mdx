GamesRadar+ has a Chapter 1 checklist for Tourist Trap, and lots of players are searching for it, because this is the mission where GTA 6 locks you in. Guides agree it begins when Jason drives to pick up Lucia from work after Recalibration, so as long as you don't make that drive, you can stay in free roam. Once it starts, Tourist Trap leads straight into Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time, and you don't get free roam back until Chapter 2.

Here's what you can lose. According to other guides covering the same mission (linked below), the end of the chapter takes the cash you're carrying, so put it in the Family Savings account first. One guide suggests moving at least $5,000. You also lose Jason's Key Lento house, and Shirley's two side errands expire. Story missions can't be replayed, so make a manual save in a separate slot before you start. We couldn't load GamesRadar's page ourselves, so the specifics above come from that other coverage.
