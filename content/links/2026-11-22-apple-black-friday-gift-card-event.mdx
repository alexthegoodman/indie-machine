Apple's Black Friday sale works the way it has for years: no price cuts, just an Apple Gift Card when you buy an eligible product, from November 27 through November 30. The maximum card by category is $300 for a Mac, $125 for an iPad, $100 for an iPhone or Apple Watch, $75 for AirPods or a Beats accessory, $50 for HomePod and $25 for accessories. Products shown include the iPhone Air, iPad Pro, MacBook Pro, Apple Watch Ultra 4 and AirPods Max 2.

All of those are "up to" amounts, and the fine print matters. Carrier-connected purchases and Apple Upgrade purchases don't qualify, and the card value "may vary by Eligible Product". The offer only applies at qualifying locations while supplies last, and the details live on a separate terms page. If you were going to buy at full price anyway, the gift card is a real rebate. If not, a retailer's straight discount on the same Mac is often worth more than credit you can only spend at Apple. Apple also lists trade-in credit, carrier deals and 3% Daily Cash on Apple Card.
