NordVPN's Black Friday page is running its usual end-of-year discount on two-year plans with three bonus months, so you pay 27 months up front. Deal sites don't agree on the exact price, which suggests Nord changed it during the sale. Basic is reported at $2.99 to $3.49 a month, Complete at $3.99 a month (78% off, about $107.73 up front), and Prime at $6.99 a month. One site says the deal runs until December 9.

The renewal price is what to plan for. The discount only covers the first term, and Basic is reported to renew at about $139 a year, much more than the sale price. If you sign up, set a reminder before the term ends so you can renegotiate or cancel instead of being charged the full rate. Nord's page blocks automated readers, so we couldn't check prices on it directly, and the figures above come from the deal coverage linked below. The checkout page has the current price.
