Red Bull's guide explains the Criminal Profile, GTA 6's version of Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system. It tracks how violent you are, how often things end lethally, and whether civilians and animals get hurt, and it ties into Wanted and Heat levels. Jason and Lucia each have their own profile. There are five tiers. **Professional** and **Hardened** charge Focus with stealth and non-lethal takedowns and still let you surrender to police. **Violent** charges Focus on headshots. **Psycho** (heavy violence against cops, civilians and animals, though hunting doesn't count) removes the option to surrender.

The fifth tier is the one to watch. Stay Psycho too long and you drop to **Irredeemable**. It has no passive way to charge Focus and can't be undone for that character for the rest of the playthrough. Rockstar says it affects the finale, though the guide says nobody has fully mapped how yet. You can move freely between the other four tiers. Finishing off downed enemies pushes you toward Violent, and driving over gravestones or killing animals pushes you toward Psycho. To check where you stand, press L1/LB on the map for the "Jason & Lucia" tab, or hold right on the D-pad. The guide doesn't say how long counts as "too long" in Psycho, so keep an eye on the icon.
