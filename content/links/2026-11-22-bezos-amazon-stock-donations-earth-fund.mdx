An SEC filing dated November 16 shows Jeff Bezos giving away 771,492 Amazon shares: 674,088 on November 12 and 97,404 on November 13. Fortune, citing Forbes's math at a $257.75 close, puts the total at $198.9 million. The smaller gift, about $25 million, went to the Bezos Earth Fund Foundation, which has filed to sell. Those particular shares date back to July 5, 1994, the day Bezos incorporated the company that became Amazon. That's where the headline's "held since 1994" comes from. It doesn't describe the whole $198.9 million.

Counting gifts in May and August, Bezos has given at least 2.44 million shares this year, about $629.1 million at that price. It still barely dents his stake of more than 878 million shares (about $226.4 billion), since this year's gifts come to under 0.3% of it. The Earth Fund has pledged $10 billion by 2030 and had granted about $2.4 billion by late September, so it has to spend roughly $1.8 billion a year from here. On November 19 it announced more than $100 million in grants for the Amazon rainforest. Day 1 Academies, the tuition-free preschool network that has received at least 450,837 shares this year, runs 38 schools in six states.
