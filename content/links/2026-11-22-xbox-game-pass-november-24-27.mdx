Pure Xbox lists the three games arriving on Game Pass to end the month. On November 24, **MOTORSLICE** moves down from Ultimate to the Premium tier. **Sniper Ghost Warrior Contracts 2** arrives the same day on every tier except Essential (Ultimate, PC and Premium), and the listing mentions its Butcher's Banquet expansion, which adds a new region called The Temple.

The day-one game is **Arkheron**, arriving November 27 on Ultimate and PC Game Pass. It calls itself a "fast-paced, clip-worthy team PvP game" for squads of three, and its official account said it entered Early Access on October 29. Pure Xbox doesn't say whether the Game Pass version is still the Early Access build or which platforms (console, PC, cloud) each game supports. Check the Xbox app listing before you plan a weekend around one. These three are part of a larger November 18 to December 1 lineup that Microsoft announced earlier.
