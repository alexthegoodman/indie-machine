Map Genie is a long-running site for interactive game maps, and its Leonida map is where a lot of GTA 6 players go to look up locations: collectibles, points of interest and side content that's easy to miss in a state this size. Like the site's maps for other open-world games, it's built to be kept open in a second tab while you play.

One thing to know: before launch, most "GTA 6 maps" were fan reconstructions pieced together from trailers, screenshots and leaks, like the State of Leonida project. Some of those still rank well in search. If a map you find labels areas from pre-release guesses, it's out of date. Now that the game is out, use one that's being updated from real playthroughs. We couldn't load Map Genie's page to check how complete its coverage is right now, so treat this as a pointer, not a review. If you'd rather read than click around, PC Gamer's tour of the map (linked below) covers the regions.
