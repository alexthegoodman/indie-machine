Yahoo Finance executive editor Brian Sozzi's Sunday column covers two big AI stories from the same week. Anthropic listed on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, priced at $83 a share, and raised $81 billion. That passes SpaceX's $75 billion as the largest IPO on record, and Sozzi puts the valuation at $1.2 trillion. The stock opened Thursday at $111.50 and had dropped to $88 by Friday's close. That is still above the offer price, but a lot of the first-day pop was gone. He also says the press was kept out of the Nasdaq booth on listing day.

The Nvidia half is the odder one. Sozzi says founder Jensen Huang "is gone" and that the company is in a leadership crisis during its earnings week. Interim CEO Colette Kress ran her first all-hands without him on Wednesday, promised raises and more stock, and didn't take employee questions directly. The column doesn't say how or why Huang left, and it doesn't explain where the $1.2 trillion figure comes from (one of Yahoo's own linked live blogs says $1.5 trillion). Treat it as a quick read on the mood, and wait for the filings and Nvidia's own statements before relying on the details.
