This month's full moon, the Beaver Moon, is full on November 24, 2026. timeanddate.com puts the exact moment at 9:53 am Eastern; convert for your own time zone. The site links it to infographics calling it a "Super Beaver Moon", but the main text doesn't say how much bigger or brighter it will look. If you want to see it, the evenings either side of the full moon are just as good.

The name has a couple of explanations. Some sources say it's when beavers are building their winter dams, and since beavers are mostly nocturnal, they'd be working by the light of a full moon. Other traditional names include Frost Moon, Freezing Moon and Trading Moon, and in Celtic tradition the last full moon before the December solstice is the Mourning Moon. Every few years the November full moon becomes the Hunter's Moon instead, when the Harvest Moon falls early in October, as it did in 2025. Next year's Beaver Moon is November 13, 2027.
