The desktop pet genre has a long and storied history on personal computers. In the late 1990s and early 2000s, applications like Neko the desktop kitten, BonziBuddy, and screen mates roamed desktop wallpapers, offering quirky ambient companionship. Yet as modern operating systems grew more complex, desktop companions largely vanished, often undone by clunky window layering, intrusive dialog popups, or accidental mouse clicks that derailed productive work.

Indie developer Alexframe is breathing fresh life into this nostalgic sub-genre with *Gramps & Whiskers* (Steam App ID: 5515850), a free-to-play desktop companion game designed specifically for modern Windows environments.

The game introduces an irresistible character dynamic: Gramps, a grumpy, weathered old fisherman who perches directly on your Windows taskbar with his fishing rod, and Whiskers, an mischievous ginger tabby cat who traverses the frames of your open windows, naps on Gramps' lap, and sneaks fish straight out of his tackle bucket while he dozes off.

Engineered with complete click-through transparency and Steam Inventory integration for collectible cosmetic gear, *Gramps & Whiskers* delivers a warm, whimsical dose of slice-of-life charm to everyday workstation routines.

## Workspace Ergonomics: The Click-Through Imperative

Anyone who has tried running desktop pets while writing code, editing video, or answering emails knows the primary frustration: accidental misclicks. You click to focus a text editor or hit a browser tab, and instead you accidentally grab a 2D sprite, bringing work to a jarring halt.

Alexframe solved this issue from the ground up by engineering *Gramps & Whiskers* with native Windows layered window transparency (`WS_EX_TRANSPARENT` and `WS_EX_LAYERED`). Every left-click, right-click, double-click, and mouse drag passes directly through Gramps and Whiskers into whatever application is running beneath them. 

You can type code right over Gramps' fishing line, drag browser tabs directly through Whiskers as she lounges on an edge, and highlight text without the characters intercepting your cursor. When you actually want to interact with the companion app, an unobtrusive icon lives quietly in your system tray, letting you open settings, inspect your tackle box, dress up your characters, or toggle sound volume.

Furthermore, CPU and GPU overhead is virtually nonexistent. By utilizing lightweight 2D frame rendering and smart sleep polling when no movement is detected, the application consumes only a few megabytes of resident memory, ensuring it never competes for system resources.

## The Dynamic: A Grumpy Angler and a Thief with Paws

The heart of *Gramps & Whiskers* lies in the interplay between its two titular characters:

- **Gramps on the Taskbar:** Gramps sits comfortably on the edge of your taskbar, casting his line down into the invisible waters below your monitor bezel. He is an old sea dog with an endless supply of dry humor. As you work, Gramps mutters about the weather, grumbles when he reels in old rubber boots and seaweed, and reacts comically if you leave your PC idle for long periods (often falling asleep with his hat pulled down over his eyes, snoring in small pixel z's).
- **Whiskers on Your Windows:** Whiskers is driven by an autonomous pathfinding state machine. She detects the bounding boxes of your open application windows in real time. If you move a window across your screen, Whiskers rides along on top of the title bar, balances delicately along window borders, bats at the edges of your scrollbars, or leaps down to the taskbar to curl up and purr on Gramps' lap.
- **The Bucket Heist:** When Gramps successfully reels in a fish and drops it into his galvanized steel bucket, the cat's AI perks up. If Gramps turns his head or falls asleep, Whiskers will sneak up, paw a prize trout out of the bucket, and scramble up to the nearest window ledge to devour it. When Gramps wakes up to find an empty bucket, his expressive grumbles are a constant source of amusement.

## Steam Inventory Integration: Catching Rare Cosmetic Gear

While the primary joy of *Gramps & Whiskers* is ambient observation, Alexframe integrated a clever collection loop tied directly into the Steam Community ecosystem.

Instead of ordinary fish, Gramps' rod will periodically hook glowing treasure chests containing cosmetic wearable items:
- **Five Rarity Tiers:** Equipment ranges from Common and Uncommon up to Rare, Epic, and Legendary.
- **Customizable Outfits:** Items include fisherman raincoats, nautical beanies, tweed flat caps, aviator goggles, cozy woolen scarves, monocles, pirate eyepatches, and miniature sweaters for Whiskers.
- **Steam Inventory Support:** Items drop directly into your native Steam Inventory. Players can trade duplicates with friends on the Steam Community Market, collect seasonal holiday accessories, and customize both Gramps and Whiskers with unique aesthetic combinations.
