The classic cooperative round-based survival formula pioneered by early *Call of Duty: Zombies* remains one of the most exhilarating multiplayer experiences in gaming history. The frantic rush of boarding up windows, purchasing weapons off plaster walls, training swarms of enemies around tight corners, and desperately upgrading pea-shooters in arcane punch machines created friendships and shattered headsets for a generation. Yet in recent years, mainstream shooters have largely abandoned this focused, arcade-first wave loop in pursuit of sprawling extraction modes and live-service battle passes.

Indie development collective Paths Systems is reviving that frantic arcade magic with a gloriously unhinged comedic twist in *Sploink* (Steam App ID: 5420300), a free-to-play cooperative wave shooter releasing tomorrow, November 17, on Steam.

In *Sploink*, up to eight players step into the webbed feet of armed combat ducks, barricading farmhouses and fortresses against twenty-five relentless waves of psychotic enemy waterfowl before confronting the apocalyptic Great Honk. 

Featuring 55 absurd firearms, interactive physical hazards, glide mechanics, and an upgrade machine called the Quack-a-Punch, *Sploink* is an unapologetic love letter to round-based co-op survival.

## Holding the Pond: Barricades, Roof Camping, and Gliding

Survival in *Sploink* begins with fortifying your immediate perimeter. Spread across eight distinctive battlegrounds, including storm-swept shipping docks, a desert fortress, lakeside log cabins, an active volcanic forge, and a multi-story farmhouse, the maps are specifically constructed around defensible choke points and vertical escapes.

Defensive teamwork is mandatory from round one:
- **Boarding Windows and Doors:** As swarms of crazed ducks sprint toward your position, players must hammer wooden planks across entryways. The flock will aggressively peck barricades apart, forcing defenders to actively repair broken planks under heavy fire to keep corridors sealed.
- **Verticality and Glide Physics:** When a defensive line inevitably collapses, ducks can take to the air. Holding the jump key deploys your wings into a sustained aerodynamic glide, allowing players to leap off second-story windows, float across alleyways, and escape onto neighboring rooftops to regroup.
- **Interactive Terrain Hazards:** The environment dynamically impacts movement and combat physics. Players and enemies alike slide uncontrollably across icy ponds, trudge slowly through sticky mud pits, splash through rivers, and risk instantaneous cremation by getting knocked into bubbling lava vats.

## An Absurdist Arsenal: From Pea Shooters to the Bee-M-G

Paths Systems has delivered one of the most creative and delightfully ridiculous arsenals ever conceived for an indie shooter. With 55 distinct weapons purchasable off walls or scavenged from airborne care packages, loadout experimentation is endlessly entertaining.

Firearms range from straightforward ballistic firepower to slapstick madness:
- **Exotic Ballistics:** Weapons include the Pea Shooter, the booming Honk Cannon, energy-based Duck Rays, and the terrifying Bee-M-G (which fires swarms of enraged homing bees that track moving waterfowl).
- **Trick Shots & Rocket Jumps:** Certain shotguns fire pellets that ricochet around three right-angle corners; cryogenic blasters freeze enemies into solid ice cubes that can be kicked into crowds; high-explosive ordnance allows players to rocket-jump directly onto church steeples; and Cupid rays cause hostile ducks to fall head-over-heels in love with your squad, turning their weapons against their own flock.
- **The Quack-a-Punch:** The centerpiece of squad progression is the legendary Quack-a-Punch station. For a hefty sum of round-earned feathers and cash, players can feed their favorite firearms into the machine up to three times, transforming humble sidearms into laser-spewing, multi-barreled instruments of catastrophic destruction.
- **Bill Headshots:** Precision aiming is heavily rewarded. Striking enemy waterfowl directly on the bill triggers critical damage multipliers accompanied by hilarious audio squawks.

When personal firearms are not enough, players can deploy automated turrets, lob explosive egg grenades, call in air-support Goose Strikes, or radio for parachute squads of friendly rifle, breacher, and medic ducks that drop from the sky to provide escort cover.

## The Flock: 14 Enemy Types and the AI Director

To ensure that 25-wave survival never turns into predictable target practice, *Sploink* employs an active AI Director that adjusts enemy spawn compositions based on your squad's current health, ammo reserves, and defensive position.

The opposing waterfowl legion boasts 14 specialized archetypes:
1. **Sprinters & Tanks:** Fast-moving ducklings charge ahead to overwhelm choke points, followed by heavily armored brute ducks that soak up heavy machine-gun fire.
2. **Specialist Waterfowl:** Explosive suicide bombers trigger deadly chain reactions if eliminated in close quarters; claymore ducks waddle into doorways to lay landmines; spitters lob toxic goo from elevated perches; and bubble ducks generate protective kinetic shields over surrounding infantry.
3. **Burrowers & Airborne Gulls:** Just when your barricades feel impenetrable, burrowing ducks tunnel beneath your floorboards, while shrieking seagulls dive-bomb players exposed on rooftop vantage points.
4. **The Juggernaut Goose:** Every fifth wave culminates in a terrifying boss encounter against a massive, armored Juggernaut Goose equipped with telegraphed charge attacks that can level wooden barricades in a single blow.

Between wave sets, the flock temporarily migrates, allowing your squad to vote on travel routes across branching road maps where each path trades economic bounties against environmental hazards.

## PvP Duel Mode and Player Respect

When your squad has conquered the 25-wave campaign and tackled the post-game Endless mode, *Sploink* includes a dedicated "Duel" PvP mode. Players enter the arena with identical weapon loadouts and friendly fire set to maximum, culminating in dramatic slow-motion final showdowns to crown the ultimate duck.

Best of all, Paths Systems has committed to total player respect: the game is 100% free to play, with every single cosmetic hat, vest, and feather coloration unlocked purely through gameplay milestones and secret achievements, completely devoid of microtransactions.
